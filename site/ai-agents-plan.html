05 The Learning Loop Event-Triggered
Honest Expectation ElevenLabs agents do not self-train. Nothing improves on its own. What works is a managed evaluation loop — Claude reads the call data, finds what wins, rewrites Blake's prompt. The trigger is 50+ new transcripts, not a calendar cron — when there's enough new data to learn from, the loop fires.
Step 1

Capture

Every call's transcript, recording, and outcome flow in via ElevenLabs' post-call webhook.

ElevenLabs → CF Worker → APG-Vault
Step 2

Label

Outcome written to GHL (stage move + tags) and a dataset file: booked / objection / hung-up / not-interested.

GHL API + calls.jsonl
Step 3

Analyze

At 50+ new transcripts the loop fires. Claude reads the batch and finds what the booked calls did differently.

Event-triggered, NOT cron
Step 4

Refine

Claude outputs concrete edits to Blake's system prompt + objection playbook + knowledge base. v3.1 → v4.0 → v4.1 …

Vault: _system/Blake/iterations/
Step 5

A/B Test

Refined prompt deploys to ~20–30% of calls as the new version; booking lift measured vs control.

ElevenLabs versioning
Step 6

Promote

Winner becomes current. Loser archived. Loop repeats — Blake sharpens with every batch.

Archive: _system/Blake/prompts-archive/

07 Compliance Non-Negotiable

This part carries real legal exposure. Every item must be cleared before Blake touches a live list.

!
Voice-clone consent (Adam)

Adam completes ElevenLabs' in-product consent recording before any voice clone fires on a call. The meeting agreement is the green light; the in-app step is the legal record. Get it in writing too.

!
AI disclosure

The FCC ruled AI-generated voices in calls fall under TCPA. Several states require disclosing the caller is AI. Blake introduces himself as "Blake — partner with Atom Property Group"; AI status is disclosed when asked or when state law requires.

!
Hand-off language locked

Blake hands off to "RJ, my acquisitions partner" — never "specialist." Adam explicitly forbade that word. Hand-off phrasing is part of the system prompt and tested on every Blake version.

!
SMS halt holds

Outbound SMS to homeowners is halted per the May 2026 strategy shift. Only the listing-pipeline (realtor outreach) and inbound replies are live SMS surfaces. Don't re-enable homeowner outbound without a fresh DNC + TCPA review.
